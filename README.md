# Level Up

Put it online (free, about 5 minutes):

1. Make a free account at github.com and create a new public repository called `levelup`.
2. Choose "uploading an existing file" and drag in every file from this folder.
3. In the repository, open Settings, then Pages. Under "Branch" pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then Save.
4. After a minute your app is at `https://<your-username>.github.io/levelup/`.
5. Open that link in Safari on your iPhone, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

Your data lives only on your phone. Use History, then Export, every week or so.
